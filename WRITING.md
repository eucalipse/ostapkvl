# Writing rules (ostapkvl.com articles)

Articles live in `content/posts/<slug>.mdx`. Images live in `public/writing/<slug>/`.

## Images
- **Every image carries the watermark `ostapkvl.com`** (bottom-right, small, muted). Charts and diagrams are
  generated by `scripts/figures/build.mjs`, which adds it automatically -- add new figures there, not by hand.
- **Every image has alt text**, written as a sentence that says what the image shows and what the reader should
  take from it (not "chart" or "diagram"). Use the `<Figure src alt caption />` component; it refuses to render
  without a real alt text.
- Numbers on illustrative charts are illustrative. Say so in the caption.

## Content
- No legal / law-firm topics, examples or clients (NDA). Use neutral examples: suppliers, logistics, ESG, operations.
- No internal product or module names, no internal metrics (record counts, client counts, accuracy numbers from
  internal evals, commit or line counts). Approved public numbers are listed in the site handoff notes.
- Plain, human English: short sentences, concrete examples, first person where it is my experience. No
  "in today's fast-paced world", no "delve", no lists of buzzwords. One idea per paragraph.
- New articles start with `draft: true` in the frontmatter. Drafts render in `next dev` and never in production.
  Remove the flag only when the owner says publish.
