## 5 highest-priority corrections

1. **Settle employer and client confidentiality before anything goes public.** Five posts repeat "60+ actions / 13 integrations at a US professional-services firm" and describe internal incidents: a dead API key, a deactivated bot, colleagues' habits. Given your NDA, check whether that wording and those details could identify your employer or its industry. Use the employer detail in one post at most, or drop it.
2. **Fix the privacy exposure in four posts:**
   - vector-memory: the tester's grief, the pet's name and the dated record, plus consent for emotional profiling.
   - 500k: patient health data in emails.
   - agent-observability: context snapshots kept with no retention policy.
   - three-models: client documents sent to three vendors.
3. **Make the shared production system tell one story.** Promotion rules differ across observability, approval-gates and shadow-mode. Whether the stale email was sent or caught differs between observability and approval-gates. The marketplace kill switch is "global" in one post and "too coarse" in the other. Readers who read more than one post will notice, so write one canonical rollout description and reuse it.
4. **Support or soften the first-person metrics**, starting with shadow-mode, then 500k, sixty-actions, three-models and marketplace-agents (87k listings). If you have internal data, say so. If not, round the numbers and mark them as approximate. Remove absolute claims such as "never had to be switched off".
5. **Fix source overclaiming and the templated figures.**
   - Replace or narrow the Du et al. citation in three-models.
   - Check the Anthropic source-control figures in review-the-decision.
   - Add the missing citations: n-version programming, emotional-memory research.
   - Replace the 15 boilerplate figures with diagrams specific to each article, or remove them.

**Separate design fix:** in marketplace-agents-cost-guards, the auto-delisting path doesn't check that the sender owns the business. If that's true in production, it's a real bug, not just a writing issue.
